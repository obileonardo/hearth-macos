# Community conduct

Keep discussions respectful, constructive and focused on the project. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats and deliberate disruption are not welcome.

Respect other people’s privacy. Never publish credentials, household information, private camera footage or sensitive security reports in public discussions. Follow SECURITY.md for vulnerability reports.

Report conduct concerns privately to contact@elvisobi.com with enough context for the maintainer to review them. The maintainer may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to keep the project usable and welcoming.
